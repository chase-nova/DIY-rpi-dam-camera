# Contributing

Thanks for helping other people build their DAM camera! Fixes, clearer
steps, better photos and reports of what worked (or didn't) on your
hardware are all welcome.

## Ways to help

- **Found a mistake or got stuck?** Open an issue with the **Docs
  problem** form — say which step and what happened.
- **Built one with different parts?** Use the **Hardware report** form:
  Pi model, camera, lens, box, and how it went (heat, focus, Wi-Fi).
- **Want to fix it yourself?** Edit `README.md` and open a pull request.
  Keep the style: short numbered steps, commands in code blocks, one
  idea per paragraph.

## Photos

Photos make this guide. If you add one:

- **Your own photo**, which you agree to publish under the guide's
  license (CC BY 4.0).
- **No personal details**: no faces, house numbers, car plates, readable
  addresses, or views into private homes or gardens.
- **Strip location data**: remove EXIF/GPS metadata before committing
  (most phones: share → options → location off; or any EXIF remover).
- **Web size**: JPEG, about **1600 px** on the long side, well under
  1 MB. Put it in `images/` with a short descriptive name
  (`pi-zero-in-box.jpg`) and show it in the README with
  `<img src="images/NAME.jpg" alt="what it shows" width="600">` plus a
  one-line caption.
- Screenshots of the web app: use a test account with test data.

## Safety

Don't suggest mains wiring inside the camera box, and keep the privacy
advice in section 11: cameras should film scenery, not people or
neighbours.
